Kick vs Bass · coming soon

Room for the kick. Weight for the bass.

Kick vs Bass turns the bass down only on the kick's frequencies, and only when the kick hits. No added latency.

Included in the pass · 70 € a year How it works

Set up in three steps.

01

Put it on the kick

Load Kick vs Bass on your kick track and choose THIS IS THE KICK.

02

Put it on the bass

Load it on your bass track too. The two find each other on their own: the window shows KICK LINKED.

03

Choose who wins

Turn I WANT MORE KICK or I WANT MORE BASS. Classic sidechain routing still works if your DAW needs it.

Two knobs, or every detail.

Normal

I WANT MORE KICK and I WANT MORE BASS set everything for you. More kick doesn't make the kick louder: it digs the bass deeper when the kick hits.

More options

  • Reduction: the dip where the kick sits
  • Pump: ducks the whole bass, like a classic sidechain
  • Attack, Hold, Release
  • Width and Gain

On / Off

Compare with the original bass at any time. VST3 for Windows and Mac, Audio Unit for Mac.
