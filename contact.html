Frequent questions.

Which formats do the plug-ins come in?

VST3 for Windows and Mac, and Audio Unit for Mac.

Windows says the installer is unknown. What do I do?

Click "More info", then "Run anyway". The full steps are on the download page.

My DAW doesn't show the plug-in.

Rescan your plug-ins in your DAW's settings, then restart it. If it still doesn't show, email us with your DAW and your system.

What does the pass include?

Every La Pause plug-in, today's and the new ones released during your year, for 70 € a year. See the pass page.
